# Brag Plan: Upgrade Myself — AI & Vibe-Coding Learning Studio

## What is this app?
Upgrade Myself is a 16-week learning studio for AI, vibe-coding, full-stack systems, and design vocabulary featuring 128 concepts, 12 interactive visual labs, a daily study habit loop, and 100% offline PWA + Android support.

## The angle
Stop memorizing ephemeral syntax that AI tools can write for you. Build deep mental models and system vocabulary behind AI engineering, LLM architectures, context engineering, agents, full-stack web, and UX laws.

## Hook (first 0–4 seconds)
Laser strike-through on "STOP MEMORIZING SYNTAX" with deep sub-bass impact, transitioning immediately to "UPGRADE YOUR MENTAL MODELS & SYSTEMS".

## Key moments (the middle)
- **16-Week Roadmap & 4 Pillars:** 128 concepts across Foundations, Context & Retrieval, Agents & Vibe-Coding, and Full-Stack Systems.
- **Interactive Visual Labs:** Hands-on 3D Vector Embedding Space, Transformer Attention visualizer, and live Tokenizer.
- **Daily Habit Loop & 100% Offline PWA:** 20 min weekdays / 2h weekends study system, works completely offline on Web and Android with custom document import (PDF/MD/JSON).

## Outro / punchline
"UPGRADE MYSELF" neon badge with glowing aurora background: "Master AI Systems & Vibe-Coding in 16 Weeks. Install PWA or Android · Start Free".

## User flow worth showing
1. Hook: Syntax trap vs Mental Models
