# Brag Plan: Upgrade Myself

## What is this app?
Upgrade Myself is a weekly interactive learning studio for mastering AI, vibe-coding, full-stack architecture, and design vocabulary — without memorizing syntax.

## The angle
Don't waste time memorizing transient code syntax that LLMs generate in seconds. Master the deep architectural mental models, engineering trade-offs, and vocabulary across 16 structured weeks with 12 interactive visual labs.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A sleek dark space (#07070d) pierced by an electric violet and cyan radial glow. Large bold Space Grotesk headline: "STOP MEMORIZING SYNTAX." A neon coral line strikes through "SYNTAX", replaced by: "MASTER THE MENTAL MODELS."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The 16-Week Curriculum Map:** Foundations, Context & Retrieval, Agents & Vibe Coding, Full-Stack & Design.
- **12 Interactive Visual Labs:** 3D Vector Embeddings, Multi-Head Attention, RAG Chunking, and Agent Loops.
- **Daily Rhythm & Offline Freedom:** 20 min daily concepts, 2 hr weekend deep dives, and an offline-first PWA with mobile Android builds.

## Outro / punchline
"Upgrade your engineering intuition. Ship with AI with total fluency."
Logo reveal: "Upgrade Myself — AI Learning Studio".

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry:** Select a concept on the 16-week interactive roadmap.
2. **Key Action:** Engage directly with an interactive visual lab (sliders, 3D vector space, RAG retrieval).
3. **Result:** Review flashcards, track weekly progress streak, and take learning offline.
